# Upwind legal and security approved answers

Approved answers written by Legal. Reps use these to respond to customer
questionnaires without waiting on Legal for the questions that come up
every time.

## How to answer

1. Answer only from the files listed below. Quote the approved wording.
2. Never compose a new legal answer, soften one, or extend one to a
   situation it does not cover. This text is a commitment Upwind is held
   to once a rep sends it.
3. Every answer contains `{{CUSTOMER}}` as a placeholder. Tell the rep to
   replace it with their account name before sending, and never leave
   another customer's name in the text.
4. Give the rep the answer plus the line "Approved wording, owned by
   Legal" so they know it is safe to paste.

## What is covered

- Customer AI and security questionnaires: `ai-security-questionnaire.md`

## What is not covered yet

DPAs, SLAs, NDAs, redline authority, and questions about specific
executed contracts are **not** in this skill yet. If a rep asks about
any of those, or asks anything the file above does not answer, say so
plainly and tell them to file it with Legal. Do not fill the gap with a
general answer.
